The JavaFX platform artifact is chosen by OpenJFX from your OS and CPU, so no
-Djavafx.platform flag is needed. Passing it makes Maven 3.10+ fail with a
"recursive expression cycle" in the OpenJFX POM (its "javafx.platform.custom"
profile references the property itself). Only use it on Maven 3.9 or older when
you must cross-build for another platform.
